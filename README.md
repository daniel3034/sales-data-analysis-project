# Overview

As a software engineer expanding my data analysis skills, I'm building this project to learn how to extract meaningful insights from real-world sales data using Python and the pandas library. This hands-on experience will strengthen my ability to work with datasets, perform statistical analysis, and create visualizations that communicate findings effectively.

This project analyzes sales transaction data from a retail business spanning 2014-2017. The dataset includes 9,994 transactions across multiple product categories, with information about order dates, product categories, sales amounts, quantities, profits, and regional data.

**Dataset:** Superstore Sales Dataset from Kaggle with 9,994 records covering 48 months of sales data across 3 product categories (Technology, Furniture, and Office Supplies).

**Dataset Link:** https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/vivek468/superstore-dataset-final

The purpose of this software is to demonstrate proficiency in data manipulation, statistical analysis, and visualization by answering specific business questions through code. This project showcases skills in filtering, sorting, grouping, aggregating data, and converting data types - all essential capabilities for data-driven decision making.

[Software Demo Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1rAzH-96hfo)

# Data Analysis Results

**Question 1: Which are the top 3 product categories by revenue?**

1. **Technology**: $836,154.03 (36.4%)
2. **Furniture**: $741,999.80 (32.3%)
3. **Office Supplies**: $719,047.03 (31.3%)

**Analysis:** The three product categories show relatively balanced revenue distribution, with Technology leading slightly. Technology accounts for about 36% of total revenue, while Furniture and Office Supplies contribute approximately 32% and 31% respectively. This balanced portfolio suggests diversified revenue streams across product lines.

**Question 2: How do monthly sales change over the last four years (2014-2017)?**

- **Average monthly sales**: $47,858.35
- **Highest performing month**: November 2017 with $118,447.82
- **Lowest performing month**: February 2014 with $4,519.89
- **Trend**: Sales show strong upward growth from 2014 to 2017, with significant seasonal variation and year-end peaks

**Analysis:** The data reveals substantial growth over the 4-year period, with monthly sales increasing from around $4,500 in early 2014 to over $118,000 in late 2017. This represents approximately 25x growth, indicating strong business expansion. The pronounced seasonal patterns suggest opportunities for strategic inventory and marketing planning around peak periods.
